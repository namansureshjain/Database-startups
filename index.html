About the mandal

A festival that leaves something behind

Every year the lanes fill with dhol, the mandap glows till midnight, and Bappa comes home to all of us. We wanted the celebration to carry more than sound.

So the collection box works differently here. Nothing is spent on the mandal itself — decoration, lighting and prasad are covered by the members from their own pockets. What visitors give is counted publicly on the last day and handed over, in full, to our chosen cause.

Come for the darshan. Stay for the aarti. Give whatever feels right — even ₹11 adds up when a whole neighbourhood gives it.

Every single day

Darshan & aarti timings

The mandap stays open right through the day. Aarti is the busiest — reach ten minutes early.

  1. 05:30 am

    Kakad Aarti

    The first aarti of the day, before the lane wakes up. Quiet, and the best darshan you will get.

  2. 07:00 am

    Darshan opens

    Open queue, no pass needed. Ramp access on the left side of the mandap.

  3. 12:00 pm

    Madhyan Aarti & Mahaprasad

    Prasad is served till it lasts. Volunteers in saffron scarves will point you to the queue.

  4. 04:00 pm

    Cultural programme

    Different every evening — see the programme below.

  5. 07:30 pm

    Sandhya Aarti

    The one to come for. Dhol-tasha, full mandap, and the lamps lit together.

  6. 10:30 pm

    Shej Aarti · mandap closes

    Last darshan of the night. Weekends run half an hour longer.

Day by day

Programme 2026

Everything is free and open to all. Evening events start at 4:00 pm unless noted.

Day 1Mon, 14 Sep

Pratishthapana

Idol installation at the sthapana muhurat, followed by the first Maha Aarti.

Day 2Tue, 15 Sep

Atharvashirsha Pathan

Collective recitation. Everyone welcome — copies of the text are handed out.

Day 3Wed, 16 Sep

Children's day

Drawing, fancy dress and shloka contests for the under-14s. Prizes after the evening aarti.

Day 4Thu, 17 Sep

Bhajan sandhya

Local bhajani mandals through the evening.

Day 5Fri, 18 Sep

Health & blood-donation camp

Free check-ups from 9 am. Registration at the volunteer desk.

Day 6Sat, 19 Sep

Dhol-tasha pathak

The loudest night of the week. Come early, the lane fills up fast.

Day 7Sun, 20 Sep

Mahaprasad

Community lunch from noon. Bring the family.

Day 8Mon, 21 Sep

Cultural night

Dance and music by the neighbourhood's own performers.

Day 9Tue, 22 Sep

Senior citizens' felicitation

Honouring the elders who have run this mandal for decades.

Day 10Wed, 23 Sep

Satyanarayan Puja

Puja in the morning, mahaprasad in the evening.

Day 11Fri, 25 Sep

Visarjan · Anant Chaturdashi

Miravnuk leaves the mandap at 2 pm. The donation total is announced and handed over before we leave.

Dates follow the panchang for 2026 — please confirm with the mandal before travelling.

Where your money goes

Anna Daan & school-kit drive

100% of what we collect is passed on — nothing is kept by the mandal.

  • Decoration, lighting, sound and prasad are paid for by the members themselves.
  • The donation box and this UPI ID feed a single account, used for nothing else.
  • The running total is written on the board at the mandap every evening.
  • The final amount is announced on visarjan day, in front of everyone present.
  • The transfer receipt is posted on this page within a week of the festival.

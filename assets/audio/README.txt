Put your festival music here as  aarti.mp3

  assets/audio/aarti.mp3

Use audio you actually have the rights to — a recording your own mandal made,
a track you have licensed, or something released under a Creative Commons
licence. Popular film or album versions of Ganpati songs are copyrighted and
can get the site taken down.

Keep it small (under ~4 MB) so the page stays quick on mobile data. A 2-3
minute aarti that loops is plenty.

If this file is missing, the music button still works: the page synthesises a
soft temple-bell and drone ambience in the browser instead.
